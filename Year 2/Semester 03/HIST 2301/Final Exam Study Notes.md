## **Early Canada (Confederation → 1914)**

Canada formed through **Confederation (1867)** as a compromise between regions, creating a **strong federal system (Macdonald’s design)**.

Key features:

- **Economic expansion (National Policy)**:
    - Protective tariffs
    - CPR railway (coast-to-coast integration)
    - Western settlement + immigration
- Goal: build a **self-sustaining national economy**

Major consequences:

- **Indigenous dispossession**:
    - Rupert’s Land purchase without consent
    - Numbered Treaties (different interpretations: “sharing” vs “surrender”)
    - Residential Schools (cultural assimilation + destruction)
- **Regional tensions**:
    - Maritimes felt ignored economically
    - West developed as resource + grain economy
- **Federal dominance**:
    - Strong central government over provinces (trade, rail, law, Indigenous policy)

---

## **Wartime Canada (WWI → Great Depression → WWII era transition)**

### **World War I**

- Canada enters automatically through Britain (Dominion status)
- Massive contribution (CEF)
- **Key division: English vs French Canada**
    - Conscription Crisis (1917)
- State expansion:
    - War Measures Act → strong federal power, civil liberties suspended
- Social change:
    - Women prove industrial capability → suffrage expansion

### **Interwar + Great Depression**

- Economic collapse due to:
    - Stock speculation crash (1929)
    - Global trade collapse
    - Wheat dependence (prairie crisis)
- Outcomes:
    - Massive unemployment + poverty
    - Rise of **third parties (CCF, Social Credit)**
    - Political shift: loss of faith in 2-party system

---

## **Post-War Canada (WWII → Cold War → 1970s)**

### **Cold War era**

- Canada becomes a **middle power**
- Aligns with US-led Western bloc:
    - NATO (1949)
    - NORAD (1957)
- Increased continental integration + defence cooperation
- Rising concern about sovereignty (US influence)

### **Welfare state expansion**

Post-war governments (King → St. Laurent → Pearson → Diefenbaker) create:

- Old Age Security
- Unemployment Insurance
- Canada Pension Plan
- Medicare (Pearson era)

➡️ Canada becomes a **modern welfare state**

---

### **Quebec + Trudeau era**

- **Quiet Revolution (1960s)**:
    - Secularization (Church loses power)
    - Hydro-Québec nationalization
    - Rise of Quebec nationalism
- Political responses:
    1. Federal bilingualism (Trudeau)
    2. Special status for Quebec
    3. Sovereignty movement (Parti Québécois)

### **October Crisis (1970)**

- FLQ kidnaps Cross + Laporte
- Trudeau invokes **War Measures Act**
- Laporte is killed
- Civil liberties suspended → mass arrests
- Long-term: decline of violent separatism, rise of constitutional politics
